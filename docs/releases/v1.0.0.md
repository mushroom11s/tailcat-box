## v1.0.0

First major release of Tailcat Box（猫砂盆）— a Tailcat desktop client for macOS and Windows.

### Highlights / 亮点
- Chat rooms: temporary and persistent keyed rooms, lobby create/restart, identity bar that collapses when a peer connects（聊天房间：临时房与固定密钥房，大厅创建/重启，连上对方后身份栏自动收起）
- 喵传 (Mew Share): P2P file share with 共享 / 下载 tabs, multi-share, short codes, QR with logo, multi-download with progress and resume, shares kept across restart（喵传：点对点传文件，共享/下载分页，多份分享、短码、带 logo 二维码，多任务下载进度与断点续传，重启后仍保留分享）
- Tunnel: save and manage local port mappings（穿透：保存并管理本机端口映射）
- QR share and scan for room/tunnel keys; paste or decode QR from images and the clipboard（房间/穿透密钥二维码分享与扫描；支持图片与剪贴板粘贴解码）
- Operational errors as top-right toasts（操作错误用右上角 toast 提示）
- Installers: Windows NSIS `.exe` and macOS `.dmg`（安装包：Windows NSIS `.exe` 与 macOS `.dmg`）

### Since v0.3.0 / 相对 v0.3.0
- 喵传: multi-share UI, packing/running cat loading, receive progress, resume, persist shares + keep listed while listen retries, 共享/下载 tab names（喵传：多份分享、打包/奔跑猫加载、接收进度与续传、分享持久化且监听未就绪时仍留在列表）
- Lobby: hide create/restart/connect buttons while a room is starting; restart opens another keyed room（大厅：创建/重启/连接进行中隐藏按钮；重启房间在固定密钥下再开一间）
- Room QR center logo; decode logo QR screenshots and transparent images（房间二维码中心 logo；能解带 logo / 透明底的二维码图）
- Sidebar: full active border/glow (not clipped), clearer icon/label layout, vertical spacing（侧栏：选中描边完整不裁切，图标与文字布局更清晰）
- Default landing on 喵传; remove Diagnostics panel from Settings（默认进入喵传；设置里去掉诊断页）

Binaries are unsigned. Gatekeeper / SmartScreen warnings are expected.
未签名构建；macOS Gatekeeper / Windows SmartScreen 警告属正常。
